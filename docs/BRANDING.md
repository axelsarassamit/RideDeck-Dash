# RideDeck Dash icon

The icon preserves RideDeck's exact existing dark background, blue R/road paths and white road markings from app/src/main/res/drawable/ridedeck_icon.xml in the standard RideDeck project. A small outlined camera sits in the open space below the R bowl to distinguish Dash without replacing the original mark. No Yamaha-specific identity is added.

The Android vector is the source of truth: app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_dash.xml. Preserve the three original RideDeck paths when refining the camera detail.
