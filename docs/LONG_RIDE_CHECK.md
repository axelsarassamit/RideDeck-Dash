# Long ride recording check

Use this after installing 0.8.1. Recording and playback checks do not establish outdoor stabilization quality. The next footage review will cover that separately.

## Before leaving

1. Mount the phone securely in the same position used for the ride. Set the angle while parked, using the live preview. Keep the road visible above the cockpit and windscreen edges.
2. Use **Camera selection > Rear camera focus > Road: distant focus locked**. This keeps autofocus from hunting toward mirrors and screens when the selected camera supports manual focus. Dash now refuses an unsupported focus lock instead of quietly selecting autofocus. Fixed-focus cameras remain supported.
3. Keep **Rear priority** for dual recording. If you want to compare camera load, make one separate ride with Rear only using the same rear quality. Do not change camera settings while moving.
4. Connect the charger or confirm at least 50% battery. Choose sufficient storage, clip size and recording reserve in setup. Protected clips occupy the recording budget until you remove/export them.
5. Start your RideDeck or Yamaha ride session and confirm Dash shows recording. Use the startup preview to check the angle. Background recording needs that active companion session.

## During the ride

Keep the mount position consistent and leave the app controls alone while moving. Include ordinary roads, rougher sections and a few parked or stopped sections. Do not manufacture abrupt manoeuvres for testing.

## After stopping

1. Stop the ride/recording and wait for clips to finish saving.
2. In Dash **Recordings > Back up originals and ride logs**, select the date and time window. Keep Rear camera only selected for a rear-camera review. Use an end time one minute after your last requested minute to include that minute in full.
3. Save the originals and logs, while powered and with enough free space for separate copies. Stay on that screen until it reports completion. Complete originals are copied, including footage crossing the chosen boundaries; source clips are kept.
4. Keep the backup folder and its manifest. Include capture.csv and route.csv from each ride. New capture logs contain actual focus, focal length, zoom, exposure/frame timing and thermal samples where the camera supplies them.
5. Tell us the approximate times of visible skipping, blur, focus changes or strong vibration, and whether recording was rear-only or dual. Keep original clips. Stabilized exports cannot restore missing source frames or already blurred detail.

## Social editor

Open a clip's **Social video export** action. Set the in/out points or use the current playhead, apply the trim, then play it back. Choose platform format, fit/crop, recorded sound or mute and overlays through the settings button. Portrait places controls below the preview; landscape places them alongside it. The three saved app settings slots remain available.

**Create edited copy** uses the frozen settings shown in the editor and saves a separate video to Movies/RideDeckDash/Social. The RideDeck logo stays on the copy. Nothing is posted automatically. Stabilization starts off for a new editor session; its preview/export choices are experimental pending road-footage validation. Preview shows recorded audio/mute and the route trace; export-only wind filtering and downloaded road context can differ.

This is a single-clip editor. Music, titles, transitions and multi-clip assembly are not included in this version.
